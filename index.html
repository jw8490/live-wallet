SSA Index Methodology

What is the SSA Ecosystem Cap?

The SSA Ecosystem Cap is the combined value of the companies that own the 35 tracked platforms, counting each company once: real market caps for public companies plus the latest publicly reported valuation for private ones. Instagram and Facebook count once as Meta; YouTube is Alphabet; Twitch is Amazon; Grok, Grokipedia and X are SpaceX (which acquired xAI); ChatGPT is OpenAI.

Methodology

How the SSA index powers Smart Basket.

Ecosystem Cap
Sum of the owning companies, each counted once: public market caps + last reported private valuations.
Daily change
Real change of the public companies' combined market cap vs. the previous close. Private valuations have no daily price.
Data refresh
A GitHub Action fetches quotes every 30 min on weekdays during US market hours, plus daily. The page re-checks every 5 min.
Basket
Simulated. Holdings follow the real share-price change of their owner since allocation. No securities are bought.
Data note · Public market caps are total equity value across share classes as reported by Nasdaq at the last trade (delayed, not tick-by-tick). Private valuations are the latest publicly reported figures and can be months or years old — each one links to its source. The basket, PayPal flow and SSA Proceeds are simulations. Not financial advice.
